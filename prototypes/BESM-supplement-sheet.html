Kage no Shinobi
Ability & Combat Reference
Attributes
Extra Actions Level 3
Kage moves through combat in bursts of unnatural speed, his body blurring between moments as though the shadows lend him fractions of time others cannot perceive. Against a single opponent he is relentless — blink and he has already struck twice more.
Combat Technique Level 4
Years of clan training have carved precise killing forms into Kage's muscle memory. Each strike flows from the last with mechanical economy — no wasted motion, no telegraphed intent. Opponents who survive his first exchange rarely understand what technique undid them.
Extra Arms Level 2
In the space between heartbeats, Kage's shadow extends beyond him, lending phantom leverage to his strikes and catches. What appears to be an impossible angle of attack or an inexplicable parry is the shadow acting an instant before the man.
Skills Level 2
The Crimson Clan did not train Kage only in the art of killing. They gave him languages, maps, and the reading of men's faces and loyalties. This breadth of knowledge surfaces only when needed, and never draws attention to itself.
Defects
Blind Fury Rank 2
There is a point past which Kage stops calculating. When something he protects is threatened — a clanmate, a child — a cold rage rises that narrows his world to the threat in front of him. He does not become reckless, but he becomes uncompromising, and he will take injuries without registering them until the fury passes.
Impaired Speech Rank 2
A wound to the throat during his trials left his voice permanently rough and sustained speech painful. He communicates in clipped phrases, gestures, and silence. Those who know him have learned to read a great deal into very little. Those who don't often mistake brevity for contempt.
Obligated Rank 3
Kage belongs to the Crimson Clan in a way that goes beyond employment or loyalty. They raised him. They made him what he is. When the Clan calls, he answers — regardless of what else is on the table. This has cost him before. It will cost him again.
Skeleton in the Closet Rank 1
There is one assignment Kage completed that he has never spoken of, not even in confession. The target was not who he was told they were. He completed the contract anyway. The knowledge of what he did lives in him like a stone that will not settle.
Combat Quick-Reference
Attack & Defence
Making an attack
Roll 2d6 + Attack CV vs. defender's 2d6 + Defence CV. Attacker wins ties.
Called shot
+3 to attack roll. Attacker declares target location before rolling.
Multiple targets
−3 to attack roll per additional target beyond the first.
Unarmed attack
Damage Multiplier reduced by 2 (minimum 1) unless a relevant attribute applies.
Damage & Shock
Dealing damage
Roll 2d6 + Damage Multiplier, subtract target's Armor. Remainder is HP lost.
Shock
If damage dealt in a single hit equals or exceeds target's Body score, target is Shocked and loses their next action.
Condition thresholds
Battered 75% · Injured 50% · Serious 25% · Critical 10% · Near Death 1 HP
Action Types
Standard action
Attack, activate a major attribute, full move. One per turn unless Extra Actions applies.
Sub-action
Short move, draw or stow a weapon, activate a minor or passive attribute effect.
Free action
Speak a short phrase, drop a held item, toggle an ongoing effect already in place.
Extra Actions
Each level grants one additional standard action per round at −2 CV cumulative.
